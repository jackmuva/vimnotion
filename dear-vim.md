# Vim, A Love Letter

Dear Vim,

From the moment

I saw you on ThePrimeagen's stream

How could I have known:

That I would fall in love with you.



From fumbling around

On ItelliJ's Vim plugin

To finally working up the courage

To talk to you

I kickstart(.nvim)ed my relationship

With you. And only you.



As the months went by

I programmed more and more.

Not because I was imparted

By the agility and grace you gave me

But by a new found love for my tools.



While this may come off as parody

There's truth in every line.

You made me feel like a brand new developer.

And I'll always love you for it.



For how can you love what you do

Without loving how you do it?



So when I'm building VimNotion

A Vim-first notes app

With my Neovim IDE,

It's a labor of love. 



And no matter what I'm building,

I now know one thing for certain:

I'll love how I'm building

With you by side.



:wq,
\- Jack
